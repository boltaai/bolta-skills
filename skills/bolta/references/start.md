# Start

Get someone running from their goal: useful Bolta routines, optional account, brand and voice setup, one question about anything dated; saved off, switched on with a yes. Use for: set me up, get going, I have nothing set up, keep me posting with nothing running yet.

## Done when

They understand the proposed work and any real prerequisites. What they chose is saved; on only after its own yes. Missing optional setup is not a failure.

## Know this

**Use what they supplied; ask only what the requested work needs.** Read together, answer once:

1. **Look once** (the Bolta skill), plus `list-routines`, `list-content-campaigns`, `list-business-dna`, the `list-inbox-items` pending count; load `plan`. Routines on and drafts flowing: [today](today.md).
2. **Accounts where needed.** Compatible review-only drafts can start with none. Offer `get-connect-link` for publishing or when requested. Existing accounts: bind the selected usable ones. Required source, history and company-page routines still need their account or source; never substitute another brand's.
3. **Optional brand and voice.** Reuse what exists; their goal, instructions and real facts can guide compatible drafts without either. Offer [brand setup](start-brand-setup.md) or show an existing voice ([first session](start-first-session-voice.md)). Preview before any setup save; no mandatory website or voice detour. Proof and founder stories need real results or events, never product facts alone.
4. **Start with one best-fit Bolta routine**, before any yes, in their words. Propose several only when they want several distinct jobs. Honor named networks; show any proposed network clearly. Instagram needs a picture or video ([start-matching.md](start-matching.md)).
   - Call `plan`: `kind: "routine"` for one, or `kind: "recommend"` for several requested jobs; their words as `ask`, what Bolta holds and what they told you as `context`, said first in one "Based on..." line ([routine-planner-context.md](routine-planner-context.md)). Show the plan's name, day, time, networks, drafts per run and `why`.
   - Only if `plan` is an unknown tool: `list-routine-templates` once; pick available rows and preserve their defaults. Honor stated cadence and networks; show any proposed choices for confirmation, never silently copy web defaults or invent bindings.
5. **Size to review time:** advice: 3-6 drafts a week in all; honor their stated cadence. 10+ waiting: name the count, offer to go through them first. Say "Every draft waits for your yes."
6. **One closing question, with the launch in it, one sentence, one question mark:** "Which should I set up (saved switched off; nothing runs until you say so), and is a launch, sale or event coming up I should plan for?" A yes with a date: one campaign plan (`plan` `kind: "campaign"`, `context`, pages as `links`; else [campaign](campaign.md)). Already named: plan it as its own numbered line. A launch is never a routine.

**Question budget:** at most 2 questions a reply. Ask only what blocks a valid proposal, especially missing purpose or factual material. Show proposed cadence and network together for confirmation. A website is optional.

**Saving:** a yes saves them switched off; switching on is its own yes ([start-saving.md](start-saving.md)).

**After:** "On Monday, say 'what's waiting'." Words: [first session](start-first-session.md).
