# Voice rules or Bolta settings

Read before saving a rule like "no emojis", "always mention X" or "write in Spanish".

- "No emojis", "always mention X", "write in Spanish" go into `donts` or `dos` as soft rules.
- The emoji switch, language, persona and brand-wide Shared instructions are in Bolta under Brand > Edit voice; language belongs at brand level.
- A length per network is a platform rule in Bolta: a DO is not a cap.
- Reply rules: Shared instructions > Reply guidelines.

## Always

- Follow the house rules in the Bolta skill.
- Never promise a length from a DO alone.
