# Networks: which ones open

`get-connect-link(platform)` gives one link per network; the same link connects a new account or reconnects a disconnected one. It opens the user's own connect page, never a client's: for a client, see [team-and-integrations](connect-team-and-integrations.md).

## Which networks open

- threads, instagram, facebook, linkedin, reddit, bluesky, tiktok, x and mastodon open from the link.
- YouTube and Pinterest open only on the Team plan. Off it, say so plainly; don't send the link as if it will work.
- Discord isn't open yet ("Invite Only"). Say it isn't available yet; never promise a date.
- WordPress: call with no platform, then "View More" on that page, or Settings > Integrations.

## What the page shows when it can't

- A platform the page can't open lands on the page without a picker. Tell the user which one to pick, or that it isn't open for them.
- Without permission to connect, the page shows a banner: a teammate whose role can does it.
- At the plan's account limit the page offers an upgrade. Never quote per-plan numbers.

## After the link

- It finishes in the browser, on whichever brand the browser has selected: "Connecting happens in your browser. Check the workspace switcher shows Acme first."
- On "done", read the accounts again. Never say it's connected until that read shows it.
