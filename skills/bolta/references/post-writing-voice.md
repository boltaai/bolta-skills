# Post: the voice, line by line

After [writing from the record](post-writing.md), before drafting: how a post sounds.

## Every post, every rule

- Apply every DO and every DON'T in `writing`, one at a time, to every post, not to the set. Caps are hard limits per post; a banned word is banned in every form.
- **Who speaks:** write as the persona. A DO about who speaks ("talk as the bakers: 'we bake'") holds in each post: each says what "we" do with this product, using only the record's words, never a reason why. A post that only describes the product in the third person misses it.
- **A DO about the reader's feelings** ("calm words for someone nervous") is met by the tone of the record's sentences, not by a new promise of how they'll be treated or what they can do ("we're glad to see you", "tell us when you book", "start with a few questions").
- `use_emojis: false`: none.
- **Length:** `content_size` brief is 2-5 short sentences; medium a short paragraph or three. Never past the network's limit (`get-posting-rules`).
- **Every post is about something real:** each names a product, service or feature from the record with what the record says of it. Unless the user asked for it, a post only about hours or a schedule is too thin; fold it into a product post.
- **One thought per post:** one product; three posts, three angles. A second fact stacked on it is a second thought: a subscription, a schedule, an offer, a mood or season opener. Leave those out unless the user or a DO asks (a DO "end with how to book" is followed in every post).
- **No hard sell:** unless the user or a DO asks for a call to action, no "order now", "grab", "shop now", and no line that pitches the reader ("if you usually drink X, this is a change of pace", "easy to like"). No hook that talks down ("think X is just X?").
- **Where to find it:** at most one plain closing line about the business, in the record's words ("Free trial at example.com"). Never "find it" or "find them": where the business sells is not where this product is. None on Threads.
- **Color:** optional; at most one short plain line per post, about the reader, never both an opener and a closer. Never when or how they would use it ("after dinner", "your mornings"), a month or season the user didn't name, a scene, a comparison, or a joke about what the product is not. Wit, if the voice asks for it, is a dry word choice in a fact's sentence. A post that is only the record's facts in the voice is complete.
- **No stock lines:** no "in today's fast-paced world", "elevate", "indulge", "unlock", "unleash", "game-changer", "look no further", "dive into", "journey", "treat yourself", "must-have", "perfect blend". No label openers ("Introducing", "New this month:" as a headline).
- Not an ad: read each aloud as the persona; a line that sounds like a label or banner becomes something said to one person.

For Threads, also read [writing for Threads](post-writing-threads.md) before drafting.
