# Strategy playbooks

Read when the user wants a proven shape for a campaign. These are guidance from Bolta's teaching material, not Bolta settings: say them as advice. Each names its strategy from [strategies.md](campaign-strategies.md). Its posts a week go in a text `content_mix` ([settings-formats.md](campaign-settings-formats.md)).

- **Product launch:** Problem / Solution, 3 weeks × 4 posts = 12. Arc in Context: "Week 1: the problem, no product. Week 2: the fix. Week 3: results." Link the launch page, not the homepage. Mistake: launching with Offer & Promotion.
- **Promotion with a deadline:** Offer & Promotion, 1-2 weeks × 4 posts = 4-8. Real deadline, price and offer in Context. Mistake: running past the offer.
- **Thought leadership:** Thought Leadership, 4 weeks × 3 posts = 12. About your business filled in, a source page, real positions in Context. Mistake: an empty workspace.
- **Consistency test (new):** Quick Tips or Product Education, 2 weeks × 3 posts = 6, one network if unsure. Mistake: 4 weeks × 5 kinds × 3 networks.
