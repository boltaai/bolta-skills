# Web Inbox, reply rules and monitors

Part of [held-and-inbox](replies-held-and-inbox.md).

## Comments on your own posts (web Inbox)

With Premium, Bolta collects comments on the brand's own posts from Bluesky, Threads (mentions), Mastodon, LinkedIn company pages, X (daily), Facebook and Instagram. They show at bolta.ai/dashboard/inbox under Replies.

- Replies can be drafted and sent there on Instagram, Threads, Bluesky and LinkedIn company-page posts. X always uses copy and open (Bolta copies the reply and opens the post; the person pastes it). Facebook, and Instagram when Meta won't take the reply from Bolta, fall back to copy and open too. Mastodon can't send there.
- There a person can mark a comment handled (archives it), archive older ones, or skip an outgoing drafted reply.
- Archiving doesn't hide the comment on the network.
- To answer one here, ask for its link and use [answering](replies-answering.md).

## Standing rules and monitors

- Standing reply rules ("never mention price", "always sign off") belong in the brand's Custom Instructions > AI Reply Guidelines in Bolta.
- Monitors (conversations Bolta watches for you) live at bolta.ai/dashboard/routines. `run-routine-now` on an Outreach routine starts a scan now; it needs a yes.
- Auto Reply sends by itself only if the person turns it on there and the brand needs no approval. Never suggest it unprompted.

## Always

- Follow the house rules in the Bolta skill.
- Never suggest switching on Auto Reply or a monitor that sends.
