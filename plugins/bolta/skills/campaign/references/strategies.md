# Strategies

Read when picking a strategy or reading week 1. A strategy is the stance every post takes, not a topic or format. Say the dashboard name and gloss.

## The six

- **Problem / Solution** (`problem_solution`): a real frustration, then the fix. Launches, new products; the safe default. Context: specific frustrations, not categories. Mix a week: 3 images + 1 carousel.
- **Offer & Promotion** (`offer_promotion`): the deal, its price, when it ends. Only with a real offer, price and end date. Context: the whole offer. Mix: 3 images + 1 carousel + 1 text.
- **Product Education** (`product_education`): teach one thing about how it works. "People don't get it." Context: craft detail, steps, choices, why. Mix: 2 images + 2 carousels.
- **Thought Leadership** (`thought_leadership`): a position someone could disagree with. "Build authority." Context: positions written down, things they did. Mix: 2 images + 1 carousel.
- **Lifestyle** (`lifestyle`): the product inside a real moment. Wellness, beauty. Context: the moment, not the product. Mix: 4 images.
- **Quick Tips** (`quick_tips`): one tip they can use today. Starting from nothing. Context: what their audience gets wrong. Mix: 4 images + 1 carousel.

Picking:

- A launch is Problem / Solution. Offer & Promotion assumes people already know the thing: the most common mistake.
- No real price and end date: not Offer & Promotion. Bolta never invents urgency.
- "Behind the scenes", "culture", "customer stories" are topics: put them in the theme.
- How long: a first campaign 2 weeks; a launch 3, launch day in week 3; an offer 1-2, never past its end; Thought Leadership 4. Longer arcs are two campaigns, each with a verdict.

## Four playbooks (Bolta University)

- **Product launch:** Problem / Solution, 3 weeks × (3 images + 1 carousel) = 12. Arc in Context: "Week 1: the problem, no product. Week 2: the fix. Week 3: results." Link the launch page, not the homepage. Mistake: launching with Offer & Promotion.
- **Promotion with a deadline:** Offer & Promotion, 1-2 weeks × (3 images + 1 text) = 4-8. Real deadline, price and offer in Context. Mistake: running past the offer.
- **Thought leadership:** 4 weeks × (2 images + 1 carousel) = 12. About your business filled in, a source page, real positions in Context. Mistake: an empty workspace.
- **Consistency test (new):** Quick Tips or Product Education, 2 weeks × 3 images = 6, one network if unsure. Mistake: 4 weeks × 5 kinds × 3 networks.

**Context** is what the user pastes into the campaign page's notes box (two-ways.md), not a question first. Size the plan on the mix it will run: a playbook's mix is sent as `content_mix`; otherwise Bolta uses the mix above.

## Week 1: wrong strategy or thin context?

Read week 1's drafts as a set, not one by one.

- **Vague pain nobody recognizes:** no real problems to use. Product Education, or add real frustrations.
- **Confident generalities:** Thought Leadership with nothing done to stand on. Add material, or switch.
- **Posts about the brand, not the deal:** no offer in Context; put it there.
- **"7 ways" lists:** something asks for roundups; Quick Tips is one tip a post. Fix the Context.
- **Pretty scenes that sell nothing:** Lifestyle with no product moment: Product Education.
- **Explaining what nobody asked:** Product Education aimed too deep: Quick Tips.

- Switch after week 1, not week 3: `update-content-campaign` with the new `strategy_key`. Only unwritten weeks change.
- Vague everywhere is thin context; switching gives differently vague posts. Fix in order: about your business, the campaign's Context, a source page, your documents.
