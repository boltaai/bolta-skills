# First session: what to show and say

Use real names, counts and times. Skip what's done. At most one question per moment. Dates are examples.

## 1. You're in

> You're in **Acme** (New York time). Connected: LinkedIn (Jane), X (@acme). Instagram is disconnected: reconnect here <link>.

- No accounts: "Connect an account here: <link>. Check that Acme is the workspace selected in Bolta first. Say 'done' when finished."
- UTC: "Which city are you in? I'll use it for times. Set it in Bolta Settings to make it stick."

## 2. It already knows me

> **Your voice** (Acme)
> For: owners of small design studios. Tone: direct, warm, plain.
> DO: open with a real number · name the problem first · end with one question
> DON'T: hard sell · hashtags mid-post · "game-changer"
> Recent: "We lost our biggest client in March..." (LinkedIn, Oct 6, 2,140 views)
> Bolta has learned from 12 of your decisions.

- At 0 decisions, end with the learning line from moment 4 instead.
- No voice: "What's your website, and one thing you want to post about this week? Bolta reads your site once to set up your voice."
- No documents: "Add your one-pager or price list to Your documents in Bolta and posts get more specific."

## 3. A week, done

`post` writes 4-5 drafts, each for its network, saved in one batch with no question first. It skips networks needing an image or video, and says which. One table: #, account, full text (no days: Bolta picks them). Then:

> Saved to Acme as drafts. Nothing goes out yet. Bolta will pick exact times from your posting schedule. Schedule all 5? Or tell me what to change.

## 4. Yes in one line

They say: "Yes to 1, 3, 4. Make 2 shorter. Drop 5, too salesy." `review` does it all in one reply (one Bolta check in Claude Code and Cowork).

> Scheduled: Mon Oct 19, 9:00 am, LinkedIn; Wed Oct 21, 12:00 pm, X; Fri Oct 23, 9:00 am, LinkedIn. Post 2 is ready: schedule this version?

If a drop came with a reason, offer the rule once:

> Want me to add "no hard sell" to your DON'Ts so this stops? Every yes and edit is saved. After about 10 new ones, Bolta updates your DOs and DON'Ts overnight.

## 5. It keeps going without me

Skip if a Bolta routine is already on.

> Want Bolta to write 5 LinkedIn and X drafts every Monday at 8 am, about what Acme does? They wait for your yes. It runs on Bolta even when Claude is closed, and uses credits each run.

Name the networks they post on. On yes, `routine` sets it up from the offer's words, never this week's topic (ask me first, no Bolta check). Read the first-drafts date back; never work it out.

> On. First drafts Mon Oct 26, 8:00 am (New York). On Monday, say "what's waiting".

## 6. Launch in a sentence

Skip if a campaign is already on.

> Anything coming up in the next month: a launch, an offer, an event?

- Yes: hand to `campaign`.
- No, and they're a founder: "If you connect GitHub in Bolta, every merged release can become a build-in-public draft." Then `routine` sets it up.
- No: close.

## Close

> Your week: 3 posts scheduled, 1 waiting for your yes, 1 Bolta routine on (next drafts Monday). Ask "what needs me?" any time.

Count only what happened; stopping early, close with the state so far. Never promise to check in later.
