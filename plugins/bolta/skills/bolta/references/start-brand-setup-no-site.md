# Start: a brand with no website, and its colours

Read with [start-brand-setup.md](start-brand-setup.md) once they said there is no website, or after any setup that left colours, fonts and logo empty.

## Without a website

They said there is none: set the voice up from the findings and their notes with `create-voice-profile` ([start-making-voice.md](start-making-voice.md)): `name`, a `writing_sample` they wrote or the `tone` dials, `target_audience`, `topics`, `dos`, `donts` from their words. Preview it first the same way (assumptions as statements, one question: "Save this?"), and save on their yes. Then the colours question below, in the same reply. Say what a site would add ("about your business, your products and your colours") and that they can share one later; never ask again.

## Colours, fonts and logo

A brand set up with no website has none. After it's saved, ask once, optionally: "Have brand colours, fonts or a logo? Paste them, or share an image of your logo."

- Hex codes or font names they paste: saved as given with `update-business-dna` `fields` (`colors` as hex strings, `fonts`, `visual_aesthetics`, `logo_url`) on the brand's record (`list-business-dna`). No record: they add them on the Brand page.
- From a logo image, colours are an estimate: "Looks like #1F3A5F and #F2B705, right?" Save only after a yes. Never guess fonts from an image.
- Nothing given: leave it blank; they can add it on the Brand page or by sharing their website later. Never invent visuals.

## Always

- Never invent a colour, a font, a logo or a fact; ask once, then leave it.
