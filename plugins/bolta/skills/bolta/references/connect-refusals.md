# What this connection may do: refusals

Read after a refusal, or when someone asks what this connection can do. Back to [connections](connect-connections.md) and [connect](connect.md).

Nothing here lists what this connection may do ahead of time. A refusal names the reason (the role it needs, the plan, or the access this connection lacks): read it and say it plainly.

| The refusal names | Say |
|-|-|
| a role ("needs a workspace role of admin or owner") | "Your role can't do this." |
| the plan | "Your plan doesn't include this." |
| a permission this connection doesn't have | "This connection wasn't given that access." |
| a setting an owner or admin turned off for this person | "An owner or admin turned this off for you. Ask a brand admin; a new connection won't fix it." |

- On a sign-in connection, workspace settings are never given, by design (Bolta Settings only). Don't flag it.
- Access follows the role of whoever signed in. A viewer's connection is read-only: no saves, nothing that uses credits. A client reviewer can't connect at all. A role change applies when the sign-in next renews; sign in again to apply it now.
- A role that can't connect accounts: a teammate whose role can does it.
- Give Bolta's sentence; don't try another way.
- Owners and admins approve; a creator's approval is refused. Per-member permissions change either: trust the refusal.

## Always

- Follow the house rules in the Bolta skill.
- Never retry a refusal another way.
