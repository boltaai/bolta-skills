# Which Bolta routines fit which business

Read after [routine-templates.md](routine-templates.md) when someone asks "what should Bolta run for us?" and Bolta's planner isn't offered (with it, `recommend-routines` picks and sizes the set: [routine-planner.md](routine-planner.md)). **Bolta** marks Bolta's own grouping; the starter sets are **advice**. Offer only rows `list-routine-templates` shows as creatable and available.

## Bolta's own grouping (Bolta)

| Business | Kinds Bolta files under it (not the full list) |
|-|-|
| Salons, spas, gyms, pet and local services | `weekly_social_posts`, `customer_win_social_proof`, `repurpose_top_posts`, `thread_from_top_post`, the Square routines |
| Clinics, dental and law practices | `weekly_social_posts`, `customer_win_social_proof`, `deep_dive_series`, `weekly_blog_article`, `new_article_to_social`, the Square routines |
| Software and startup founders | `weekly_social_posts`, `founder_story_series`, `linkedin_thought_leadership`, `ship_log`, `weekly_blog_article`, `hubspot_customer_win`, reply routines |
| Consultants, coaches, agencies, creators | `founder_story_series`, `linkedin_thought_leadership`, `deep_dive_series`, `weekly_blog_article`, `repurpose_top_posts`, reply routines |

Reports fit every business. A business in none of these rows (a shop, a cafe): start from weekly posts.

## Starter sets (advice)

Each set is 3-5 Bolta routines doing different jobs, about 3-6 drafts a week in total. Reports add no review.

- **Local service** (salon, gym, groomer): weekly posts, 2 for Instagram and Facebook; customer wins, 1, only from wins the customer agreed to share; the weekly report. With Square connected, add open slots, 2.
- **Practice** (clinic, dentist, law firm): weekly posts, 2; a research series, 1, on the question patients or clients keep asking; a weekly article, 1, if they have a site; the weekly report.
- **Founder or software company**: founder stories, 2, in a person voice, from real events; LinkedIn positions, 2; the weekly report. GitHub connected: posts from each merged pull request.
- **Consultant, coach or agency**: LinkedIn positions, 2; founder stories, 1; a weekly article, 1; the weekly report.
- **Shop, cafe or restaurant**: weekly posts, 3, with an image each when Instagram is bound ([routine-outputs.md](routine-outputs.md)); the weekly report.

## Choosing within a set

- **Voice first.** Story and position routines need a person voice and real events; without them, start with weekly posts and add the story routine once the user gives events ([routine-settings-writing.md](routine-settings-writing.md)).
- **One job each.** Never two routines doing the same job on the same network; raise the first one's `count` instead.
- **Not first:** best posts again and threads from a best post need months of published posts with likes, comments or shares. Reply routines need Premium and someone who reviews replies within a day or two.
- **Dated things** (a launch, a sale, an event) are a campaign, proposed beside the set: [campaign](campaign.md).
- **Drafts already waiting:** 10 or more, say the count and offer to go through them first; if they still want more, propose at most 3 routines of at most 3 drafts, and prefer the report.
- **Then save switched off**, and switch on only on its own yes ([routine](routine.md)).

## Always

- Propose from what the business said and its site shows; never a set copied whole when one line doesn't fit.
- Say the weekly total: "About 5 drafts a week, each waiting for your yes."
