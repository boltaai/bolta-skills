# Start: which Bolta routines fit which business

Read with [start](start.md) when choosing the 3-5 to propose. Bolta's planner (`plan` with `kind: "recommend"`) picks from the brand's own posts, sources and routines; check its picks against this page, and use it directly when there is no planner. Everything here is advice except the numbers marked Bolta's. Every kind, what it needs and its limits: [routine-templates.md](routine-templates.md).

## By business

| They are | Propose first | Add if true |
|-|-|-|
| A shop, cafe, maker or local business | Weekly posts with a picture each (`weekly_social_posts`) | Customer stories (`customer_win_social_proof`) once they have real ones to share |
| A salon, studio or clinic taking bookings | Weekly posts | The Square routines, if Square is connected; customer stories with the customer's yes |
| A service or B2B company | Weekly posts; customer stories | LinkedIn positions (`linkedin_thought_leadership`) if they post on LinkedIn |
| A founder, consultant or coach | Founder stories (`founder_story_series`), in a person's voice | LinkedIn positions |
| A software or developer-tool team | Weekly posts | Posts from each merged pull request (`ship_log`), GitHub connected |
| A brand with a blog or wanting search traffic | Weekly posts | A weekly article (`weekly_blog_article`); posts from each new article, blog connected |
| A brand with months of published posts | Whatever is missing above | Best posts rewritten (`repurpose_top_posts`): never a first routine |

- Each routine is a different job. Two doing the same job on the same network is one too many.
- Stories and customer wins need real events from the user; with none to share, leave them out. Never invent one.
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest need a picture or video: pictures are set as [routine-outputs.md](routine-outputs.md) says.
- A report posts nothing and costs no review time. Offer one beside 1-2 writing routines, once there are published posts to read.
- Name them in their words: "Weekly candle care tips".

## A routine or a campaign

- No end date ("keep me posting", "every week"): a Bolta routine.
- One thing with a date (a launch, a sale, an opening, an event): one campaign, 1 to 4 weeks, as its own numbered line. Never a routine.
- Both is normal: 3 routines plus one campaign for next month's launch. Count the campaign's posts a week in the review load.

## Sizing

- Review is the bottleneck. Advice: a beginner decides about 3-6 drafts a week; start the whole set there, weekly, 2-3 drafts each. Say the total: "About 5 drafts a week."
- Daily only when they ask and already decide what they get.

## When drafts are already waiting

Read the pending count (`list-inbox-items`) before proposing.

- **10 or more waiting, or drafts made but not decided:** say the count and offer to go through them first: "You have 12 drafts waiting. Want to go through those first?" More drafts into an unread list is the wrong fix.
- **They asked what to run, or still want more:** show at most what Bolta's planner sized, reports first. Bolta's numbers: at 10+ waiting (`review_load` medium), up to 3 plans of up to 3 drafts a run; at 25+ waiting, or 10+ and over 3 times what they decide in a week (high), 2 plans of 2.
- Asked for three more with 40 waiting: at most one, and say why.

## Always

- Match the routine to the business and the networks they named.
- Name the waiting count before proposing more drafts.
