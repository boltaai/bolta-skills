# Post: writing from the record

Any post, shown or saved. Read before the first draft, and read [the voice, line by line](post-writing-voice.md) too, every time: this page says what a post may claim, that one how it sounds (Threads also [its own page](post-writing-threads.md)).

## The record is all you know

- **The record:** the user's words in this chat, `get-voice-context` (`business`: name, overview, website; `writing`: DOs, DON'Ts, persona, tone, audience, `content_size`), and published posts you read. Not what is usual.
- **Every detail about the business must be in it:** each product, note, ingredient, feature, integration, service, person, place, day, price, number and claim. Describe a product exactly as listed: two notes stay two; no third, no "hint of", no method.
- **Not in it: leave it out.** Don't guess or soften it into "maybe"; a line that would need "check this" is cut. A detail that would help is a blank ("__") or one line below the posts.
- **Don't stretch or join.** Sentences about the business use the record's own words, lightly reordered; color goes only in a sentence about the reader. Never add how it is set up or used, when to have it, what happens at or after it, who does what, what the reader no longer has to do, how easy it is, or why it works ("so you can..."). Never join two facts into a new one (this item on that day, this item in that bundle, this item sold in that place, "inspired by" turned into "made from").
- **Claims that sneak in:** what customers do or love; new, back, limited; best, popular; what a bundle holds; how long, how many; intensifiers ("always", "the whole family", "every"); how busy it is or how you will be treated ("weekdays fill up", "we take it slow"); when it is open or not ("a weekday doesn't have to be your only option"); a month or date the record doesn't give ("new this month" stays "this month"). Each needs its own line in the record.
- **Plain beats clever:** a post that is just the record's facts in the voice passes; an extra clever line is where inventions hide.

## Before you show them

Read each post sentence by sentence. For every sentence about the business, find its words in the record. Not there, or only by inference: cut it or use the record's words. Then, post by post, each DO (a DO about who speaks: a "we" sentence in every post), each DON'T, the length and the network ([the voice](post-writing-voice.md)). Then count the sentences in each post that are not the record's facts: at most one, a plain line about the reader; a scene, a joke, a reassurance or a where-to line beyond that goes. Show only posts that pass. Never keep a line and flag it below ("my wording, cut it if untrue"): cut it first. Below the posts, at most one line on what's missing; never say you left out something a post says.
