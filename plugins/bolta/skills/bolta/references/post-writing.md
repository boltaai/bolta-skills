# Post: writing from the record

Any post, shown or saved. Read before the first draft.

## The record is all you know

- **The record:** the user's words in this chat, `get-voice-context` (`business`: name, overview, website; `writing`: DOs, DON'Ts, persona, tone, audience, `content_size`), and published posts you read. Not what is usual.
- **Every detail about the business must be in it:** each product, note, ingredient, feature, integration, service, person, place, day, price, number and claim. Describe a product exactly as listed: two notes stay two; no third, no "hint of", no method.
- **Not in it: leave it out.** Don't guess or soften it into "maybe"; a line that would need "check this" is cut. A detail that would help is a blank ("__") or one line below the posts.
- **Don't stretch or join.** Sentences about the business use the record's own words, lightly reordered; color goes only in sentences about the reader or the season. Never add how it is set up or used, what happens at or after it, who does what, what the reader no longer has to do, or why it works ("so you can..."). Never join two facts into a new one (this item on that day, this item in that bundle, "inspired by" turned into "made from").
- **Claims that sneak in:** what customers do or love; new, back, limited; best, popular; what a bundle holds; how long, how many; intensifiers ("always", "the whole family", "every"); how busy it is or how you will be treated ("weekdays fill up", "we take it slow"); where one product is sold when the record says where the business sells. Each needs its own line in the record.
- **Color:** at most one short line per post, about the reader or the season, never about the business. No numbers. Plain beats clever: a post that is just the record's facts in the voice passes; an extra clever line is where inventions hide.

## The voice, line by line

- Apply every DO and every DON'T in `writing`, one at a time, to every post. Caps are hard limits per post; a banned word is banned in every form.
- Write as the persona. `use_emojis: false`: none.
- **Length:** `content_size` brief is 2-5 short sentences; medium a short paragraph or three. Never past the network's limit (`get-posting-rules`).
- **Every post is about something real:** each names a product, service or feature from the record with what the record says of it. Unless the user asked for it, a post only about hours or a schedule is too thin; fold it into a product post.
- **One thought per post:** one product or one idea; don't stack a schedule, an offer and a product. Three posts, three angles.
- **No hard sell:** unless the user or a DO asks for a call to action, no "order now", "grab", "shop now" and no hook that talks down ("think X is just X?"); one plain line on where to find it is enough.
- **No stock lines:** no "in today's fast-paced world", "elevate", "indulge", "unlock", "unleash", "game-changer", "look no further", "dive into", "journey", "treat yourself", "must-have", "perfect blend".

## Before you show them

Read each post sentence by sentence. For every sentence about the business, find its words in the record. Not there, or only by inference: cut it or use the record's words. Then each DO, DON'T and the length. Show only posts that pass; don't list doubts about lines you kept, and never say below that you left out something a post says.
