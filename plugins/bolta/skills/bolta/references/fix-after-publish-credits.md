# Out of credits

Read with [fix-after-publish.md](fix-after-publish.md). A skipped routine run does not come back, but the routine and its jobs restart on their own.

- Bolta routine runs are skipped and every Bolta agent job pauses; they resume on their own once credits are back.
- A campaign is parked and rechecked every few hours; after a top-up, Generate now in Bolta tries at once.
- Top up at [bolta.ai/dashboard/settings/billing](https://bolta.ai/dashboard/settings/billing), then read the routine ([routine](routine.md)) or campaign ([campaign](campaign.md)) again.
- Say it plainly: "Nothing is broken. Your routines and campaign are waiting for credits. Top up and they pick up again; a run skipped while you were out won't be written."

## Always

- Follow the house rules in the Bolta skill.
- Never tell them a routine or campaign is broken when it is only waiting for credits.
