# Your documents, in Sources

Read before mentioning documents, a price list or a one-pager. Setting up the voice itself is [voice-setup-and-documents](voice-setup-and-documents.md).

Sources in Bolta (bolta.ai/dashboard/knowledge-base?tab=references) reads PDFs (a scanned one up to 100 pages), Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), text, Markdown and images up to 25 MB each, and audio or video up to 200 MB. A Dropbox folder can sync there too.

## What to say

Once, near the end of setting up, unless they already keep files there:

> Add your one-pager or price list to Sources in Bolta and posts get more specific: bolta.ai/dashboard/knowledge-base?tab=references

It helps what Bolta writes for you while nobody is in the chat (Bolta routines, campaigns): Bolta's writers search the brand's files when they draft. A campaign uses a file once it's added as one of the campaign's sources ([campaign](campaign.md)).

## What the flags mean

- `knowledge.available` false only means no folder is linked to this voice. Bolta's writers search the brand's files anyway, so it is not a sign the brand has none.
- `knowledge.available` true: say nothing about documents unless asked.

## What you can't do from here

- You can't add or read a file from here. "Use my price list": ask them to paste the facts this post needs, and give the line above for next time.

## Always

- Follow the house rules in the Bolta skill.
- Never say a document was added or read from here.
