# Final "In your AI app" prompts

Rendered from `buildAiAppPrompt` in WEB-ai-app-panels `lib/ai-apps.ts` (feat/ai-app-empty-states, PR #2661). One prompt per workflow. DevConsole has a site on file; Harbor Physio has none.

## DevConsole (site: devconsole.example)

### Brand (485 chars)

Use Bolta to set up our brand for DevConsole. If no social accounts are connected, give me the link to connect one first. Start from our website, devconsole.example, and add what you know about me and our business from our chats and any notes or writing samples I share. Preview before saving: positioning, products, writing rules, topics and a sample post. Flag assumptions. Only after I say yes, save it, assign the voice to our connected accounts, and link me to review it in Bolta.

### Routine (637 chars)

Use Bolta to set up the right routines for DevConsole. First show me what you're basing this on, from our chats and our brand in Bolta (voice, what's already running, our best posts), then ask only for what's missing. Recommend 3 to 5 routines that fit how much I can review each week, with no duplicates of ones already running, and let Bolta pick the template, day, time and topics. For each, show me what it produces, the schedule and timezone, the account, and who reviews (me unless I say otherwise). After I pick, save them switched off with review on every post, link me to each in Bolta, and ask separately before turning any on.

### Campaign (531 chars)

Use Bolta to plan a campaign for DevConsole around our next launch or event. First show me what you're basing this on, from our chats and our brand in Bolta (voice, what's already running, our best posts), then ask only for what's missing. If I haven't said which event or the dates, ask in one question. Show me an outline: audience, key message, channels, call to action and a week-by-week plan. After I say yes, save it as a draft and link me to it in Bolta. Ask separately before starting it, and keep every post for my review.
